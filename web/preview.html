When does 2x beat 1x on IREN?

A daily-rebalanced 2x position on IREN (spot $58.00) wins only when realized volatility along the path stays below a breakeven curve that depends on the target and holding period — not on anyone's drift forecast. This page makes that surface visible and lets you slide the inputs.

06 · Method

Method & limits

  • Continuous-time identity: LT/L0 = (ST/S0)β · exp(−½(β²−β)·QV − (β−1)cT − φT).
  • Breakeven (β=2, no fees): σbe(K, T) = √(ln(K/S₀) / T).
  • Ground truth is the discrete daily rebalance simulator — closed-form is used for the interactive panel and as a continuous-limit sanity check.
  • Drift μ is not estimated (its standard error swamps the point estimate on IREN's short history). All MC statistics are conditioned on K-arrival.
  • Block bootstrap preserves IREN's fat tails and vol clustering; we also show low/base/high regimes from the rolling-σ quantiles.
